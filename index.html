Profile

My background spans biomedical engineering, materials science, and hands-on technical systems. I started with medical devices, testing, maintaining, and supporting clinical equipment, which sparked an interest in what makes physical products perform reliably.

Graduate study in Materials Science & Engineering expanded that perspective to materials, processes, and characterization, while research taught me to approach technical problems through experimentation, measurement, and data analysis. Today, at Philips Canada, I work with complex medical imaging systems, building practical experience in system verification and troubleshooting.

Across these experiences, I've become most interested in the engineering between a design and a reliable product: using testing, analysis, and validation to understand performance and make physical systems better.
